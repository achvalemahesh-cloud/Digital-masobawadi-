Masobawadi ke Vikas ke Liye Prayas

Gaon ka vikas tabhi sambhav hai jab gaon ke log, yuva aur zimmedar nagrik milkar apne gaon ki zarooraton ko samjhein aur unke samadhan ke liye sakaratmak kadam uthayein.

Hamare Vikas ke Lakshya

  • Sadak aur aane-jaane ki suvidha mein sudhar
  • Gaon mein safai aur swachhata
  • School aur bachchon ki shiksha ko protsahan
  • Street lights aur zaroori suvidhaon par dhyan
  • Gaon ke yuvaon ki bhagidari
Hamari soch: Gaon ki samasyaon ko pehchanna, un par charcha karna aur milkar samadhan ki disha mein prayas karna.
